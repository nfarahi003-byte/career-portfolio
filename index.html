👋 Hello, I'm Nurul Farahi

My name is Nurul Farahi Binti Yahaya. I was born in 2003 in Dungun, Terengganu. I am currently a final-year student in Semester 6 of the Bachelor of Information Technology (Media Informatics) programme at Universiti Sultan Zainal Abidin (UniSZA).

I am always motivated to learn new things and challenge myself through different projects. Exploring new technologies, improving my coding skills, and developing creative solutions are among the things that motivate me to continue growing in the field of web development.
